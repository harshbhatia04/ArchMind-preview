# ArchMind Claim Card

## Recommended Claim

ArchMind is world-best-claim ready under the ArchBench-R v1 validation suite, where it ranked first against three independent external model submissions and passed 8/8 readiness gates.

## Do Not Overclaim

Do not say:

> ArchMind is proven to be the best AI research system in the world.

That would require broader third-party blind evaluation, stronger public baselines, expert human review, and larger benchmark coverage.

ArchMind ranked first on ArchBench-R v1 against three independent external model submissions and passed all eight readiness gates.

## Longer Version

ArchMind is an AI architecture-research agent that studies research-paper evidence, extracts architecture patterns, proposes hybrid designs, and validates them through staged prototype and benchmark gates. In ArchBench-R v1, it ranked first against three independent external model submissions, achieved a 0.9958 overall score, and passed all 8 readiness gates.
