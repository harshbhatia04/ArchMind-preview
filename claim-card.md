# ArchMind Claim Card

## Recommended Claim

ArchMind is a world-class AI architecture-research candidate under the ArchBench-R validation program. In ArchBench-R v1, it ranked first against three independent external model submissions and passed 8/8 readiness gates. In ArchBench-R v2 Challenge, it leads the harder 27-case benchmark after the 5-submission external validation gate.

## Do Not Overclaim

Do not say:

> ArchMind is proven to be the best AI research system in the world.

That would require broader third-party blind evaluation, stronger public baselines, expert human review, and larger benchmark coverage.

## Short Version

ArchMind ranked first on ArchBench-R v1 against three independent external model submissions, passed all eight readiness gates, and leads the harder ArchBench-R v2 Challenge after five external submissions.

## Longer Version

ArchMind is an AI architecture-research agent that studies research-paper evidence, extracts architecture patterns, proposes hybrid designs, and validates them through staged prototype and benchmark gates. In ArchBench-R v1, it ranked first against three independent external model submissions, achieved a 0.9958 overall score, and passed all 8 readiness gates. In ArchBench-R v2 Challenge, a harder local follow-up suite, the reference ArchMind run scored 0.9955 across 27 cases against a 0.4741 deterministic baseline and remained rank 1 after five external model submissions.
